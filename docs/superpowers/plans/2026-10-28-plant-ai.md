# Plant AI Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Identify plants by name or photo and help sick plants (symptom guide first, Claude Haiku second), with real public-domain photos for every catalog species.

**Architecture:** Shared typed data (symptom guide, AI result schemas, harm guard) in `packages/shared`. The API owns every model call behind a `PlantAi` interface (Anthropic implementation, fake in tests), with per-user limits, a daily spend ceiling, server-held proposals and a Commons photo fetcher. The app gets the "Add with AI" tab, the symptom guide sheet, the AI check-up flow and the yellow advice box.

**Tech Stack:** Fastify 5, Prisma 7, zod 4, Vitest (API), Expo SDK 57, React Native, Jest (app), `@anthropic-ai/sdk` (new, API), `sharp` (new, API), `expo-image-manipulator` (new, app).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-plant-ai-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Model `claude-haiku-5-5` (env `AI_MODEL`, default that id). Pricing constants: US$0.10 input / US$0.50 output per million tokens.
- Identification: thinking `{ type: 'disabled' }`, effort `low`. Check-ups: adaptive thinking, effort `low`. Structured outputs through the SDK (`output_config.format`), validated with the shared zod schemas.
- Limits per user per UTC day: 10 identifications, 5 check-ups. A catalog hit doesn't count. `AI_DAILY_SPEND_LIMIT_USD` default `0.50`; `0` turns AI off. No `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` also means off.
- Proposals expire after 24 h.
- Photos sent to the model: the long side is at most 1024 px, JPEG quality 0.7, resized on the phone.
- Species photos: Wikimedia Commons, CC0 or public domain only. The short side must be at least 800 px. They are cropped square, saved as 800 px WebP, and stored with source URL, author and license.
- Commons requests send `User-Agent: PlantCare/0.1 (arthur.frollini@gmail.com)`.
- All user-facing text in `en` and `pt-BR`. Model answers follow `Accept-Language`.
- The symptom guide recommends only water, light, airflow, pruning, mild liquid soap and repotting.
- Tests never call Anthropic or Commons: fakes and recorded fixtures only.
- Repo conventions: zod everywhere, `AppError` for errors, Prisma migrations, CI green (`npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`, `npm test`).

## Review Focus

- A photo that isn't a plant (a cat, a screenshot) returns "couldn't identify" or "not a plant" and creates nothing. Covered in Tasks 6 and 8.
- Two quick taps on "Add to my garden" for one proposal create one species and one plant. Covered in Task 6.
- The AI turned off (limit `0` or no key) shows clear unavailable states in the app. The symptom guide still works. Covered in Tasks 6, 11 and 12.
- The model suggests something harmful in a check-up. The guard replaces it, and nothing harmful is stored. Covered in Task 8.
- Commons has no public-domain photo, or is slow or down. "Add to my garden" still succeeds, and the species keeps the placeholder. Covered in Task 7.
